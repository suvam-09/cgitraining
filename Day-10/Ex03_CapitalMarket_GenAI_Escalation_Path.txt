Question: Sketch an Escalation Path
1. Think of a GenAl use case (real or hypothetical) relevant to your organization.
2. Sketch a simple escalation flowchart in 5 steps or fewer: who notices a problem, who they tell, and what happens next.
3. Name a specific role or person at each step - not just "governance" or "management."
4. Share your flowchart with a partner and ask them to try to break it: where would it actually fail?

Answer:
USE CASE
GenAI Investment Research Assistant

A GenAI agent summarizes earnings reports, analyst notes, and market news to generate stock recommendations for portfolio managers.

ESCALATION FLOW (5 STEPS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1] Equity Research Analyst
        |
        | Notices AI recommendation conflicts with source documents
        v
[2] Senior Research Analyst
        |
        | Validates evidence and impact
        v
[3] Portfolio Manager
        |
        | Assesses trading/risk exposure
        v
[4] AI Product Owner
        |
        | Investigates model/prompt/data issue
        v
[5] Head of Investments
	│
	└── Approve fix, suspend AI output, or mandate human-only review

ROLES & ACTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Equity Research Analyst
- Detects discrepancy between AI recommendation and source data.
- Raises incident and notifies Senior Research Analyst.

Step 2: Senior Research Analyst
- Validates issue severity.
- Escalates material investment risks.

Step 3: Portfolio Manager
- Reviews potential trading impact.
- Pauses affected recommendations if required.

Step 4: AI Product Owner
- Investigates model, prompts, and data sources.
- Applies fix, rollback, or mitigation.

Step 5: Head of Investments
- Assesses business and regulatory impact.
- Decides whether to continue, restrict, or suspend AI usage.

HITL FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI Recommendation
        	|
        	v
Human Research Analyst
        	|
   Approve?
    /    	\
  Yes			No
  |        	|
Publish  Escalate
          	v
     		Senior Analyst

BREAK THE PROCESS REVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Analyst trusts AI blindly.
2. Portfolio Manager already acted before escalation.
3. AI Product Owner not informed.
4. Missing audit trail.
5. Single point of failure in escalation path.

DESIGN RECORD FIELDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Incident ID
- Date/Time
- AI Recommendation
- Source Documents
- Issue Detected By
- Escalation Path
- Investigation Findings
- Human Decisions
- Corrective Action
- Final Resolution
